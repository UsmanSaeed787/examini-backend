Create the complete teacher journey from initiating an assessment request through curriculum analysis, blueprint approval, AI question generation, quality review, and final publishing. Integrate the existing AI Runtime, Assessment Workflow, Tool Layer, and business services into one cohesive user experience. Focus on delivering a production-quality workflow with a polished API, state management, progress tracking, approval checkpoints, error recovery, and audit history. This should become the flagship AI feature of the platform rather than a collection of disconnected endpoints.
